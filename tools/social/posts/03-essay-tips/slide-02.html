Read your opening like a judge

An example first paragraph, before edits
Everyone knows hunger is a big problem in today's world. ↳ Says nothing yet. Which prompt are you answering, and what's your answer?
Studies show that millions of people go hungry. ↳ Which study? Which page? Credit it, or cut it.
In order to To understand this issue, we must utilise use many perspectives. ↳ Short words read faster. Judges reward thinking, not long words.
Try opening like this "In this essay I argue that ______ is responsible, because ______."
